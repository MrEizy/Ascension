Perfect Ascension

Minecraft 1.21.1 • NeoForge

Perfect Ascension is a comprehensive cultivation mod that brings the world of Xianxia (Immortal cultivation) to Minecraft. Progress through cultivation realms, comprehend Dao paths, build sects, and ultimately ascend to immortality.

⬇ CurseForge ⬇ Modrinth

Overview

Ascension transforms Minecraft into a cultivation world where players start as mortals and can rise through increasingly powerful realms. The mod features a deep progression system, custom combat mechanics, alchemy, formations, and a sect management system.

💡 Tip: Start by finding Spirit Stones in caves. These are essential for beginning your cultivation journey.

Getting Started

Installation

  1. Install Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge
  2. Download Perfect Ascension from CurseForge or Modrinth
  3. Place the .jar file in your mods folder
  4. Launch the game and begin your cultivation journey!

First Steps

When you first enter the world, you'll be a mortal. To begin cultivating, you need to:

  1. Find Spirit Stones — Mine deep underground for Spirit Stone ore
  2. Craft a Meditation Mat — Use Spirit Stones and wool to create your first cultivation tool
  3. Begin Meditation — Right-click the mat to start absorbing Qi from the environment
  4. Break Through — Once your Qi is full, attempt a breakthrough to the next realm

Dao Comprehension

Dao paths represent the fundamental laws of the universe. By comprehending a Dao, you unlock unique abilities and combat techniques.

  • Dao of the Sword — Master the way of the blade with devastating sword Qi attacks
  • Dao of Alchemy — Refine powerful pills and poisons with enhanced success rates
  • Dao of Arrays — Create more powerful and complex formation arrays
  • Dao of Space — Teleportation, pocket dimensions, and spatial manipulation
  • Dao of Time — Slow enemies, accelerate growth, and glimpse the future

Items & Blocks

Spirit Stones

Spirit Stones are the primary currency and cultivation resource. They come in three grades:

  • Low-Grade Spirit Stones — Found in caves, used for basic cultivation
  • Mid-Grade Spirit Stones — Found in spiritual veins, worth 100 low-grade
  • High-Grade Spirit Stones — Extremely rare, worth 100 mid-grade

Cultivation Tools

  • Meditation Mat — Used for cultivation sessions
  • Pill Furnace — Refine pills and elixirs
  • Spirit Gathering Array — Increases ambient Qi density
  • Sect Banner — Marks sect territory

Wiki

How the System Works

The cultivation system in Ascension is based on Qi (spiritual energy). Players absorb Qi from the environment through meditation, then use it to strengthen their bodies and souls. Each realm requires a "breakthrough" — a challenging event that tests the player's accumulated power.

Breakthroughs

Breakthroughs can succeed or fail based on several factors:

  • Qi Saturation — Having 100% Qi increases success rate
  • Breakthrough Pills — Consumable items that boost success rate
  • Spiritual Veins — Cultivating near spiritual veins provides bonuses
  • Heavenly Tribulation — Higher realms trigger lightning tribulations you must survive

Combat

Ascension adds a Qi-based combat system on top of Minecraft's vanilla combat:

  • Qi Attacks — Channel Qi into ranged projectiles
  • Spiritual Sense — Detect nearby entities through walls
  • Domain — Create a field that suppresses weaker opponents
  • Techniques — Special combat abilities unlocked through Dao comprehension
