Sects Work in Progress

The Sects system is a major expansion for Perfect Ascension, introducing player-run organizations with territories, hierarchies, resources, and inter-sect warfare.

⚠️ Work in Progress: This module is currently under active development. Features described here are planned and subject to change. Stay tuned for updates!

Overview

Sects are player-created organizations that serve as the social backbone of the Ascension world. Each sect occupies territory, gathers resources, trains disciples, and competes for dominance. The system draws inspiration from traditional Xianxia novels where sects are powerful institutions that shape the cultivation world.

Sect Creation

To found a sect, a player must meet the following requirements:

  • Reach at least Foundation Establishment realm
  • Possess a Sect Foundation Token (crafted or found)
  • Place a Sect Banner to claim initial territory
  • Name the sect and choose a sect emblem

Once founded, the sect leader becomes the Sect Master and gains access to the Sect Management Interface, where they can manage all aspects of the organization.

Territory System

Sects claim territory using Boundary Stones. Territory provides several benefits:

  • Qi Enhancement — Higher ambient Qi density within territory
  • Protection — Sect formations can be placed within territory
  • Resource Generation — Spirit Stone mines and herb gardens within territory produce resources for the sect
  • Building Rights — Only sect members can build within claimed territory

Sect Warfare

Sects can declare war on each other, leading to organized PvP battles with objectives and rewards. War mechanics include siege battles, resource raids, and territory capture. The losing sect may be forced to pay tribute or cede territory.

Sect Missions

Sect leaders can create and assign missions to disciples. Missions provide contribution points that determine a disciple's rank and access to sect resources. Planned mission types include gathering, exploration, combat, and crafting missions.
