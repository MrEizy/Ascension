Formation Arrays

Minecraft 1.21.1

Formation Arrays adds a powerful system of mystical formations to Minecraft. Place formation nodes in specific patterns to create area effects ranging from defensive barriers to resource generation.

⬇ Modrinth ⬇ CurseForge

Formation Basics

Formations consist of Formation Nodes placed in specific geometric patterns. Each formation requires a Formation Core at its center and Formation Nodes at the vertices. The formation activates when all nodes are correctly placed and powered with Spirit Stones.

Components

  • Formation Core — The central block that controls the formation
  • Formation Nodes — Placed at specific positions to define the formation shape
  • Formation Flags — Optional modifiers that enhance or alter formation effects
  • Spirit Stones — Fuel source for active formations

Setting Up Formations

  1. Craft a Formation Core and the required number of Formation Nodes
  2. Place the Formation Core where you want the center of the formation
  3. Right-click the Core with a Formation Blueprint to see the node placement guide
  4. Place Formation Nodes at the highlighted positions
  5. Insert Spirit Stones into the Core to power the formation
  6. Right-click the Core to activate
💡 Tip: Use a Formation Compass to verify node placement. It will glow when pointing at a correctly placed node.

Advanced Mechanics

Formation Stacking

Multiple formations can overlap. Compatible formations stack their effects, while conflicting formations cancel each other out. Use Formation Flags to resolve conflicts.

Formation Linking

Some formations can be linked together using Formation Bridges. Linked formations share power and can trigger chain reactions for devastating combined effects.

Upgrading Formations

Formations can be upgraded by replacing standard Formation Nodes with higher-tier variants (Iron → Gold → Diamond → Netherite). Higher-tier nodes increase range, reduce power consumption, and enhance effects.
